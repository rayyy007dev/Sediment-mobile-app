SEDIMENT
A todo app where every finished task becomes a star in your sky.

ON A COMPUTER
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (your Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
3. Always open it in the same browser, because your tasks are saved in that browser.

ON A PHONE
- The easiest way is the hosted link from the chat. Open it in your phone's browser.
- For the most reliable saving, put index.html on a free static host (for example Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages),
  open that address on your phone, then use Share > Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or Menu > Add to Home screen (Android).
- Your phone and computer keep separate lists. To move tasks between them, use "Copy backup" on one device
  and "Restore backup" on the other.

TIPS
- Press Enter to add a task, tap or click the circle to finish it.
- Edit a task: double-click it on a computer, or tap its text on a phone. Press / on a computer to jump to the input.
- Finished tasks are never deleted. Tap or click a star to reopen one.
- On a phone, the "Sky" button hides the lists so you can reach every star. Tap "Tasks" to come back.
- "Note to tomorrow": write a line to your future self and click "Leave note" (or press Ctrl+Enter).
  From the next day on, your latest note appears under the greeting. Every note is kept in a list below the field.
- Backup: open "Archive and backup" and click "Copy backup", then paste the text somewhere safe.
  To restore it, click "Restore backup", paste the text and click "Restore now".
- Avoid clearing site data or cookies for this page, since that also clears saved tasks and notes.
- The fonts load from the internet. Offline, it falls back to your system fonts.
